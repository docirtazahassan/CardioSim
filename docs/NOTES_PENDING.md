- Hemodynamics: in the model, cardiac output rises with heart rate more than in vivo (72→170/min: +54% without reflexes, +44% with baroreflex). Real hearts plateau because extrathoracic veins collapse (Guyton plateau) and filling time shortens. A venous waterfall was tried (collapse 2.5–4.5 mmHg) but it broke the RV-infarct nitrate-sensitivity and Eisenmenger teaching tests, so it was not adopted. Treat tachyarrhythmia BP/CO numbers as qualitative.
